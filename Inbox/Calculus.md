Q: Evaluate: $$(\frac{f}{g})'$$
A:
$$
\frac{fg' - fg'}{g^2}
$$

Q: Evaluate: $$f(g(x))'$$
A:
$$
f'(g(x))g'(x)
$$

Q: Evaluate: $$\frac{d}{dx}~ c$$

Q: Evaluate: $$\frac{d}{dx}~ cf(x)$$
