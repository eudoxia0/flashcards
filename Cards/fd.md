C: Command: [`fd p`]

Action: [Search for pattern `p` in the current directory.]

---

C: Command: [`fd p d`]

Action: [search for pattern `p` in directory `d`.]

---

C: Command: [`fd -e e`]

Action: [search for files with extension `e` in the current directory.]

---

C: Command: [`fd -e e d`]

Action: [search for files with extension `e` in directory `d`.]

---

Q: Flag for case-insensitive search.
A: `-i`

---

Q: Flag to search only files.
A: `-t f`

---

Q: Flag to search only directories.
A: `-t d`
