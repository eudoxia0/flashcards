Term: [lithosphere]

Definition: [The rigid outermost layer of the Earth.]

C:
Term: [asthenosphere]

Definition: [The ductile layer of the Earth's upper mantle.]

Q: What is a tectonic plate?
A: A segment of the lithosphere that floats on the asthenosphere.

Q: Where does most geological activity happen?
A: At plate boundaries.

C:
Term: [plate boundary]

Definition: [The zones where tectonic plates meet.]

Q: What are the three types of plate boundary?
A: Convergent, divergent, transform.

C: At a [convergent] boundary, the two plates [move closer together].

C: At a [divergent] boundary, the two plates [move apart].

C: At a [transform] boundary, the two plates [slide past each other].
