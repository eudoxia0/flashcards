C:
Term: [monoid]

Definition: [a set $S$ with an associative binary operation $S \times S \to S$ and an identity element $e \in S$.]

---

C: The set $\N$ under addition is a monoid, where the identity is $0$.

---

C: A semigroup with an identity element is called a [monoid].

---

C: Removing the identity from a monoid gives us a [semigroup].
