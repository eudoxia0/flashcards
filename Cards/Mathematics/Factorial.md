Q: Define the factorial function symbolically.

$$\fact{n} = \ldots$$

A: $$\fact{n} = n \times \fact{n-1}$$

---

Q: Express $n!$ using product notation.
A: $$n! = \prod\limits_{i=1}^n i$$

---

Q: Evaluate: $0!$
A: $1$

---

Q: Evaluate: $1!$
A: $1$

---

A: $2$

---
