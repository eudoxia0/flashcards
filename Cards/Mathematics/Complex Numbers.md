Q: What is $i$ in Cartesian notation?
A: $$(0, 1)$$

---

Q: Geometrically, what is $\overline z$?
A: Reflecting $z$ around the $x$ axis.

---

Q: $$i$$
A: $$\sqrt{-1}$$

---

Q: Notation for the conjugate of a complex number $z$.
A: $\overline{x}$.

---

Q: Let $z \in \C$. What does $\overline{z}$ stand for?
A: The complex conjugate of $z$.

---

Q: Let $z = a+bi$. Define the complex conjugate $\overline{z}$.
A: $\overline{z} = a - bi$
