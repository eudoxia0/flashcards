Q: What is the regex for matching digits?
A: `\d`

Q: What character class does the regex `\d` match?
A: Digits

Q: What regex matches alphanumeric characters and underscores?
A: `\w`

Q: What does the regex `\w` match?
A: Alphanumeric characters plus underscores.

Q: What does the regex `$` match?
A: The end of a line.

Q: What is the regex for matching the end of a line?
A: `$`

Q: What does the regex `^` match?
A: The start of a line.

Q: What is the regex for matching the start of a line?
A: `^`
