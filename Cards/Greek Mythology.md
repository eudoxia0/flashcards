A: Polynices.

Q: Who wrote the three Theban plays?
A: Sophocles.

Q: What are the three Theban plays?
A: _Antigone_, _Oedipus Rex_, _Oedipus at Colonus_.

Q: Sort the three Theban plays into narrative order.
A: _Oedipus Rex_, _Oedipus at Colonus_, _Antigone_.

Q: Sort the three Theban plays into publication order.
A: _Antigone_, _Oedipus Rex_, _Oedipus at Colonus_.
