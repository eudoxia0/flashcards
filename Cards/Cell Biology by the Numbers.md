Q: What is the value of Avogadro's constant?
A: $$ 6.022 \times 10^{23} ~\text{mol}^{-1} $$

Q: What is the unit of Avogadro's constant?
A: $$ \text{mol}^{-1} $$

C: The amount of substance of a sample is the ratio of  [the number of elementary entities] to [Avogadro's constant].

C: The [amount of substance] of a sample, denoted $n$, is defined as:

$$
n = \frac{N}{N_A}
$$

where $N$ is [the number of elementary entities] and $N_A$ is [Avogadro's constant].

C: The SI unit for an amount of substance is the [mole].

Q: What is the name for this unit? $$\text{mol}^{-1}$$
A: Reciprocal moles.
