Q: What is the value of Avogadro's constant?
A: $$ 6.022 \times 10^{23} \text{mol}^{-1} $$

Q: What is the unit of Avogadro's constant?
A: $$ \text{mol}^{-1} $$

$$
n = \frac{N}{N_A}
$$
