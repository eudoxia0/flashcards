Q: Consider a quantum state $\alpha\ket{0} + \beta\ket{1}$. What is the result of applying the Pauli $\sigma_x$ matrix to it?
A: $$\alpha\ket{1} + \beta\ket{0}$$

---

Q: Consider a quantum state $\alpha\ket{0} + \beta\ket{1}$. What is the result of applying the Pauli $\sigma_z$ matrix to it?
A: $$\alpha\ket{0} - \beta\ket{1}$$

---

Q: Consider a quantum state $\alpha\ket{0} + \beta\ket{1}$. What is the result of applying the Pauli $\sigma_y$ matrix to it?
A: $$\alpha i \ket{1} - \beta i \ket{0}$$

---

Q: Pauli matrices have an important commutativity property. What is it?
A: Any two Pauli matrices either commute or anti-commute.
