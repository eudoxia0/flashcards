Q: How do you pronounce typst?
A: /taipst/

---

Q: File extension.
A: `.typ`

---

Q: Executable name.
A: `typst`

---

Q: Command to compile `doc.typ` into `doc.pdf`.
A: `typst compile doc.typ doc.pdf`

---

Q: Syntax: h1 heading.
A: `= I'm a heading`

---

Q: Syntax: italics.
A: `_italics_`

---

Q: Syntax: bold.
A: `*bold*`

---

Q: Marker for unordered lists.
A: `-`

---

Q: Marker for ordered lists.
A: `+`

---

Q: Syntax: image.
A: `#image("foo.jpg")`

---

Q: Syntax: generate `n` words of lorem ipsum text.
A: `#lorem(n)`

---

Q: Syntax: document title.
A: `#title[I'm a document!]`

---

Q: Syntax: define a variable `v` with a value `e`.
A: `#let v = e`

---

Q: Syntax: embed a variable `v`.
A: `#v`

---

Q: Syntax: link to a URL `u` with text `t`.
A: `#link(u)[t]`

---

Q: Syntax: single-line comment.
A: `//`

---

Q: Syntax: multi-line comment.
A: `/* ... */`
