---
name = 'The Pursuit of Truth'
---

T: observation sentence
D: a sentence directly connected to sensory experience, like "it is raining" or "blue".

---

T: occasion sentence
D: a sentence that is true in some times and places and false otherwise.

---

T: standing sentence
D: a sentence that is true in all situations.

---

T: stimulus meaning
D: the range of stimulations associated, affirmatively or negatively, with an observation sentence.

---

T: observation categorical
D: a sentence of the form "Whenever X happens, Y happens", where X and Y are observation sentences.

---

T: focal observation categorical
D: an observation categorical where both sentences are linked in referring to a common object.

---

T: free observation categorical
D: an observation categorical that is not focal.
