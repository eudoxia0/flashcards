Q: What does Quine mean by "open sentence"?
A: A sentence that contains free variables.

---

Q: What does Quine mean by "closed sentence"?
A: A sentence without free variables.

---

Q: What does Quine mean by "simple sentence"?
A: A sentence without logical connectives, only predicates and variables.

---

Q: What does Quine mean by "complex sentence"?
A: A sentence with logical connectives.
