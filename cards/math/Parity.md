C: An integer $n$ is [even] if it can be put in the form [$n = 2k$, for some integer $k$].

---

C: An integer $n$ is [odd] if it can be put in the form [$n = 2k+1$, for some integer $k$].

---

Q: $\text{even} \pm \text{even} = \ldots$
A: $\text{even}$

---

Q: Prove: $\text{even} + \text{even} = \text{even}$
A: Let $a,b$ even. Then:

$$
a+b = 2a' + 2b' = 2(a'+b')
$$

---

Q: $\text{even} \pm \text{odd} = \ldots$
A: $\text{odd}$

---

Q: Prove: $\text{even} + \text{odd} = \text{odd}$
A: Let $a$ even, $b$ odd. Then:

$$
a+b = 2a' + 2b' + 1 = 2(a'+b') + 1
$$

---

Q: $\text{odd} \pm \text{odd} = \ldots$
A: $\text{even}$

---

Q: Prove: $\text{odd} \pm \text{odd} = \text{odd}$
A: Let $a,b$ odd. Then:

$$
a+b = 2a' + 1 + 2b' + 1 = 2a' + 2b' + 2 = 2(a'+b'+1)
$$

Q: Prove: $\text{even} \times \text{odd} = \text{even}$
A: Let $a$ even, $b$ odd. Then:

$$
ab = (2a')(2b'+1) = 4a'b' + 2a' = 2(2a'b' + a')
$$

Q: $\text{odd} \times \text{odd} = \ldots$
A: $\text{odd}$

Q: Prove: $\text{odd} \times \text{odd} = \text{odd}$
A: Let $a,b$ odd. Then:

$$
ab = (2a'+1)(2b'+1) = 4a'b'+2a'+2b'+1 = 2(2a'b' + a' + b') + 1
$$
