T: monoid
D: a mathematical object made up of a set $S$ and an associative binary operation $S \times S \to S$ and an identity element $e \in S$.

---

C: The set $\N$ under addition is a monoid, where the identity is [$0$].

---

C: Removing the identity from a monoid gives us a [semigroup].

---

C: Adding an inverse to each element in a monoid yields a [group].
