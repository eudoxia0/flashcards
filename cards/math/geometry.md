D: a crescent-shaped region bounded by two circular arcs.

T: lens

D: a convex region bounded by two circular arcs.

A: The set containing $P$, $Q$, and all points between $P$ and $Q$.

C: Notation: [$A \cong B$]

Definition: [$A$ and $B$ are congruent].

---

Q: Definition of a circle.
A: Given two distinct points $O$ and $A$, the **circle with center $O$ and radius $OA$** is the set of all points $P$ such that $OP \cong OA$.
