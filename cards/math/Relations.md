---
name = "Relations"
---

T: reflexive
D: $\forall x . R(x,x)$

---

T: irreflexive
D: $\forall x . \neg R(x,x)$

---

T: symmetric
D: $\forall x,y . R(x,y) \to R(y,x)$

---

T: antisymmetric
D: $\forall x,y . R(x,y) \land R(y,x) \to x=y$

---

T: asymmetric
D: $\forall x,y . R(x,y) \to \neg R(y,x)$
