Q: Evaluate: $$(fg)'$$
A: $$f'g + fg'$$

---

Q:

$$
\left( \frac{f}{g} \right)' = \frac{\ldots}{g^2}
$$

A: $f'g - fg'$

---

Q:

$$
\left( \frac{f}{g} \right)' = \frac{f'g-fg'}{\ldots}
$$

A: $g^2$

---

Q: Evaluate: $$f(g(x))'$$
A:
$$
f'(g(x))g'(x)
$$

---

Q: Evaluate: $$\frac{d}{dx}~ c$$
A: $$0$$

---

Q: Evaluate: $$\frac{d}{dx}~ cf(x)$$
A: $$c \frac{d}{dx}~ f(x)$$

---

Q: Evaluate: $$\frac{d}{dx}~ \sin(x)$$
A: $$\cos(x)$$

---

Q: Evaluate: $$\frac{d}{dx}~ \cos(x)$$
A: $$-\sin(x)$$
