C: Term: [magma]

Definition: [a mathematical object made up of a set $S$ and a binary operation $S \times S \to S$.]

---

C: A magma is also called a [groupoid].

---

C: A groupoid is another name for a [magma].

---

C: A magma where the operation is [associative] is called a [semigroup].
