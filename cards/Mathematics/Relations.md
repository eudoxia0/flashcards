---
name = "Relations"
---

C:
Term: [reflexive]

Definition: [$\forall x . R(x,x)$]

---

C:
Term: [irreflexive]

Definition: [$\forall x . \neg R(x,x)$]

---

C:
Term: [symmetric]

Definition: [$\forall x,y . R(x,y) \to R(y,x)$]

---

C:
Term: [antisymmetric]

Definition: [$\forall x,y . R(x,y) \land R(y,x) \to x=y$]

---

C:
Term: [asymmetric]

Definition: [$\forall x,y . R(x,y) \to \neg R(y,x)$]
