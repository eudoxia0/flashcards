Q: Who was Aristagoras?
A: The tyrant of Miletus, who incited the Ionian revolt.

---

Q: Whom did Aristagoras first appeal to for support?
A: Cleomenes, king of Sparta.

---

Q: Why does Herodotus say that it is easier to fool the many than the few?
A: Aristagoras, the Tyrant of Miletus, first appealed to Cleomenes of Sparta for support. When the king sent him away, he went to Athens, and the Athenians voted for intervention.

> It seems, then, that it is easier to deceive many than one, for he could not deceive Cleomenes of Lacedaemon, one single man, but thirty thousand Athenians he could.
