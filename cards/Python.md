Q: Let `n` be an integer. Write an f-string to render it with leading zeroes, to a minimum width of two characters.
A: `f"{n:02d}"`

Q: Let `n` be an integer. What does the f-string `f"{n:02d}"` do?
A: Writes the number with leading zeroes, to a minimum width of two characters.
