Q: Wavelength of: gamma rays
A: $\leq 10 \picometer$

---

Q: Energy of: gamma rays
A: $\geq 124 \keV$

---

Q: Photons with a wavelength of $\leq 10 \picometer$ are termed?
A: gamma rays

---

Q: Photons with an energy of $\geq 124 \keV$ are termed?
A: gamma rays


---

Q: Wavelength of: x-rays
A: $10 \picometer$ to $10 \nanometer$

---

Q: Energy of: x-rays
A: $124 \eV$ to $124 \keV$

---

Q: Photons with a wavelength of $10 \picometer$ to $10 \nanometer$ are termed?
A: x-rays

---

Q: Photons with an energy of $124 \eV$ to $124 \keV$ are termed?
A: x-rays
