Q: What is phonetics?
A: The study of human speech sounds.

---

Q: What is the trachea?
A: The tube that connects the larynx to the lungs.

---

Q: What is the larynx?
A: The organ that houses the vocal cords.

---

C: The larynx connects the [pharynx] and the [trachea].

---

Q: What is the pharynx?
A: The part of the throat between the mouth and larynx.

---

Q: What is the glottis?
A: The middle region of the larynx that contains the vocal cords.

---

Q: What is the epiglottis?
A: The flap that prevents food from entering the larynx.

---

Q: What is the velum?
A: The soft tissue that makes up the top-back of the mouth.

---

Q: Term for the tissue that separates the oral and nasal cavities.
A: Velum.

---

---

Q: List the parts of the vocal tract, from top to bottom.
A: The nasal cavity, oral cavity, pharynx, and larynx.

---

Q: List the parts of the vocal tract, from bottom to top.
A: The larynx, pharynx, oral cavity, and nasal cavity.
