T: exon
D: the parts of a gene that end up coding for RNA.

---

T: RNA splicing
D: the process that removes introns and stitches together exons from a strand of pre-mRNA.

---

Q: What is _exon_ short for?
A: Expressed region.

---

Q: Eukaryotic cells can produce multiple proteins from a single gene. Explain how.

A: Transcription can choose which exons to keep and how to join them.

---

T: ontogeny
D: the developmental history of an organism within its own lifetime.
